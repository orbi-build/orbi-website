Terms of service

THE SERVICE

Issues in, reviewed deliveries out

Orbi Cloud is a hosted runner for repositories you authorize. It can read and change those repositories to perform the delivery flow described on the Cloud page: implementation, independent review and repair, exact-head merge, and release evidence. You remain responsible for your repositories, your instructions, your model provider, and reviewing the result.

SUBSCRIPTIONS

Billing and Founding coupons

Stripe handles payment, invoices, and the customer portal. A subscription renews monthly until you cancel it through that portal. The Founding coupon is 100% off for up to 10 Founding subscriptions; it applies for the life of the subscription, not to the __INCLUDED_TOKENS__ token inclusion as a separate cash payment. After the limited Founding supply is used, new subscriptions use the regular price.

Refund requests are handled case by case. Contact support@orbi.build with the invoice and reason before asking your card issuer to reverse a charge.
