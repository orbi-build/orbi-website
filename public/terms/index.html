THE SERVICE

Issues in, reviewed deliveries out

Orbi Cloud is a hosted runner for repositories you authorize. It can read and change those repositories to perform the delivery flow described on the Cloud page: implementation, independent review and repair, exact-head merge, and release evidence. You remain responsible for your repositories, your instructions, your model provider, and reviewing the result.

SUBSCRIPTIONS

Billing and founding partners

Card subscriptions are handled by Stripe, which provides invoices and the customer portal. They renew monthly or yearly, matching the interval you chose, until you cancel in that portal. Alipay and WeChat Pay are one-time payments for the period you buy and do not renew automatically. Founding partners receive 50% off forever, limited to __FOUNDING_PARTNER_LIMIT__ subscriptions. The discount applies for the life of the subscription; after those places are filled, new subscriptions use the regular price.

Refund requests are handled case by case. First message a group admin privately on Telegram with the invoice and reason. If you can’t use Telegram, our backup email is support@orbi.build. Contact us before asking your card issuer to reverse a charge.

What you may not build
