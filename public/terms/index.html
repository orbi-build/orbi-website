Terms of service

A clear contract for the hosted delivery line. By using Orbi Cloud, you agree to use it lawfully, protect your GitHub access, and pay for the subscription you choose.

THE SERVICE

Issues in, reviewed deliveries out

Orbi Cloud is a hosted runner for repositories you authorize. It can read and change those repositories to perform the delivery flow described on the Cloud page: implementation, independent review and repair, exact-head merge, and release evidence. You remain responsible for your repositories, your instructions, your model provider, and reviewing the result.

Paid plans are Solo at US$__SOLO_MONTHLY_USD__ per month with __SOLO_INCLUDED_TOKENS__ tokens, and Pro at US$__CLOUD_MONTHLY_USD__ per month with __INCLUDED_TOKENS__ tokens. The first 3 merged deliveries are free under FREE_DELIVERY_ALLOWANCE=3. Usage and plan availability can change for future subscriptions; the price and allowance shown when you subscribe govern that subscription.

SUBSCRIPTIONS

Billing and founding partners

Stripe handles payment, invoices, and the customer portal. A subscription renews monthly until you cancel it through that portal. Founding partners receive 50% off forever, limited to __FOUNDING_PARTNER_LIMIT__ subscriptions. The discount applies for the life of the subscription; after those places are filled, new subscriptions use the regular price.

Refund requests are handled case by case. Contact support@orbi.build with the invoice and reason before asking your card issuer to reverse a charge.
