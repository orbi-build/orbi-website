Orbi weekly, every issue

Larry 🍀 Orbi · 2026-11-20

Every issue of the Orbi weekly email as subscribers received it, newest first: what changed in Orbi each week, with short demos and our own numbers.

Orbi weekly is the email I send once a week about what changed in Orbi. It covers new features with a short demo, a delivery Orbi made, and numbers from the repositories where Orbi builds itself. Each issue below is the text subscribers received, dated the day it went out. Prices, free allowances and screens in older issues may have changed since.
