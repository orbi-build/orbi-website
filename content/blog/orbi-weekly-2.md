## How repo suggestions work

You can also click "Write a request" at the bottom and describe a change in one sentence. Orbi drafts it from your code.

## One test: a sentence to a merged PR in 15 minutes

On October 9 I tried "Write a request" on one of my test repos, a small to-do web page (times are Beijing time):

- **20:20** I typed: "When I refresh the page, all my to-dos are gone." Orbi read the code and wrote a draft, adding a case I'd missed: the page must not break when the browser can't store data.
- **20:24** I read the draft and clicked "Hand off to Orbi".
- **20:31** Orbi opened a PR: 3 files changed, one of them a test file.
- **20:35** Orbi's independent AI review passed, and it merged the PR. This test repo doesn't require human approval.

Your first 3 merged PRs are free, with no credit card needed. Suggestions, drafts and unmerged PRs don't count. After the 3 free merges you need Solo or Pro; see prices on the [Cloud page](https://orbi.build/cloud/?ref=blog-nl02).
